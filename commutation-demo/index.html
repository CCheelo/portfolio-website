Personal project

A party app for six people, built for one Saturday.

Before the day it is an info hub and a private survey. On the day it turns into a synced game console — about 25 games across three halls, on six phones and an optional TV.

  • Answer the survey, then skip to Saturday — your confession joins the Vault deck.
  • All ten games play: Who Wrote It?, Know Me Best, Paranoia and The Deep End in the Vault; Name That Tune, Clap Circle and Hot Seat in the Huddle; Spyfall, Drawful and the Quick-fire Quiz in the Arena.
  • Then run the awards from the Board. The rest of the crew is simulated; the real app syncs every phone live.
How it's built

Next.js + Supabase

Postgres, Realtime channels for the game engine, and row-level security so survey answers are readable only by their author — not by the other players, not by the host, not through the API.

  • The hub shows answer counts so people can chase each other — never content.
  • Answers are released by the game engine, inside a live round, on the day.
  • Art: original poster illustrations; every game tile is hand-built SVG.
