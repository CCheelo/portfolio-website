Summary Overview

Topic

Mobile money has reshaped how people across Africa save, send, and spend — reaching millions who never held a traditional bank account. This project measures that shift: how fast adoption has grown, how much value now moves through mobile wallets, and what it means for financial inclusion, especially in Zambia.

Data Sources

  • → World Bank — Global Findex Database
  • → GSMA — State of the Industry Report on Mobile Money
  • → Bank of Zambia — National Payment Systems data

Key Questions

  • → Adoption over time — how have registered and active mobile-money accounts grown?
  • → Transaction value vs GDP — how large is the mobile-money economy relative to the formal one?
  • → Inclusion gaps — account ownership by gender, urban vs rural, and income.
  • → Benchmarking — how does Zambia compare with the Sub-Saharan Africa average?

Why It Matters

In economies where bank branches are scarce, the mobile phone has become the bank. Quantifying who is being reached — and who is still left out — turns a feel-good narrative into something measurable, and gives policymakers and providers a clear picture of where the next gains in financial inclusion can come from.
