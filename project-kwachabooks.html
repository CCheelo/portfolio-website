Kwacha Books

2026 · SaaS Product Next.js · TypeScript · Supabase Demo deploying to Vercel

A full-stack SaaS for Zambian small businesses. Kwacha Books imports your Airtel, MTN and Zamtel money statements, sorts income from expenses automatically, shows your real cashflow with a 60-day forecast and your VAT / turnover-tax position, and turns sales into ZRA-ready invoices — all billed in kwacha over mobile money.

Summary Overview

The problem

Zambian small businesses run on mobile money — but most reconcile it by hand, copying Airtel and MTN statements into notebooks or spreadsheets. Since July 2024, ZRA Smart Invoice has been mandatory for VAT-registered businesses, raising the bookkeeping bar further. Owners rarely know their true cashflow or tax position until it is too late.

What it does

  • → Import a statement — upload an Airtel / MTN / Zamtel money export; columns are detected automatically whatever the format.
  • → Auto-categorise — every transaction is split into income vs expense and tagged; you only review what the rules are unsure about.
  • → Forecast cashflow — a 60-day projection of the cash balance from recent income and spending patterns, with a confidence band.
  • → Track tax — a running VAT or turnover-tax estimate for the month.
  • → Invoice & get paid — create branded, ZRA-format invoices and bill plans in kwacha over mobile money.

Built with

Next.js (App Router) and TypeScript on the front end, Tailwind CSS for the design system, Recharts for the dashboard and forecast visuals, and Supabase (Postgres + Auth + Row-Level Security) as the multi-tenant backend. Deployed on Vercel. The public demo runs entirely client-side on seeded sample data, so it works with no sign-up — the same architecture then swaps a sandbox payment provider for live mobile-money rails once the business is registered.

Why it matters

Zambia moved nearly half a trillion kwacha through mobile money in a single year, yet the tools that turn those flows into clear books, cashflow and tax answers are thin on the ground. Kwacha Books targets that gap directly — a finance-grade data product for the businesses that need it most, priced for the Zambian market.
