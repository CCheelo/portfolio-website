The Platform

Why Zambezi Futures

A single, trusted home for youth football across Southern Africa — replacing scattered spreadsheets and paper files with one verified, living record for every player.

🪪

One identity, for life

Every athlete is issued a single PRC ID — from their first U-8 session to elite competition. No duplicate entries, no lost paperwork. Academies, coaches and administrators all work from one verified source of truth.

📈

Live competition intelligence

Match results, season ratings and player statistics update across the platform in real time. Scouts and coaches follow form, minutes and development trajectories — and spot the next breakthrough talent early.

🛡️

Built for trust & safeguarding

Every registration passes admin review and medical clearance before a player is activated. Age verification, emergency contacts and academy assignment are captured up front — keeping young athletes protected and the league compliant.
