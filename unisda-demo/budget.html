Money · budget round for 2027

Yearly budget

Ceilings are calculated, not typed: last year's grant, adjusted by how much of it the department actually used. Departments submit, the committee comments and approves, and departments trim to fit.

Ceiling = last year's grant × (0.9 + 0.2 × share used), rounded to K500. A department that used all of its grant gets up to 10% more; one that used little gets less.
