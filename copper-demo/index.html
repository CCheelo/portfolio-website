Can you forecast the
price of copper?

Copper is the backbone of Zambia’s economy, so the predictability of its price matters. This lab models 6,298 daily prices the Box–Jenkins way — and finds the level behaves like a near-random walk, while the risk turns out to be very predictable indeed.
