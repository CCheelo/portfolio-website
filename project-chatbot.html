Customer Chatbot

2026 · AB Bank Zambia Python · FastAPI In development

The bank's customer chatbot: informational, lead-generating and complaint-routing — with fraud, complaint and lead flows, automatic masking of personal data, Jira hand-off to the contact centre, and a staff analytics dashboard tracking traffic, understanding, ticket speed and leads.

Try the demo chatbot Summary Overview

Summary Overview

This system belongs to AB Bank Zambia, so it is described here in words only — no code, screenshots or data from the bank are published.

The “Chat with me” button at the bottom of every page is the same design pointed at my portfolio instead of the bank: hundreds of answers written in my own voice, over 3,000 ways of phrasing small talk, a confidence threshold, follow-up memory (“tell me more”, “what tech did you use?”), and personal data masked before anything else touches the message. No generative AI, nothing stored.

Design principles

  • Not transactional and no account access — it informs, captures leads and routes complaints.
  • No generative AI in version 1. Every answer is pre-written and approved; messages are matched to one of 28 intents, and below a confidence threshold the bot says so and offers options rather than guessing.
  • Personal data is masked first — before matching, logging or storage.
  • Kill switches turn off free text, a channel or the hand-off live, with no redeploy.

How a message is handled

  1. Guards — detect and mask personal data, scan for urgent keywords such as fraud, cap input length, filter abuse.
  2. Flows — fraud, complaint, lead and branch-locator conversations run as small state machines.
  3. Matcher — character n-gram and fuzzy scoring picks the intent, with confidence thresholds.
  4. Response — the approved answer plus follow-up buttons.
  5. Hand-off — fraud, complaints and call-backs become tickets pushed to the contact centre's Jira, with the masked transcript.

Quality and operations

  • A regression suite covers phrase coverage, dead ends, personal-data masking and red-team inputs; content edits are deploys and must pass it.
  • A staff analytics dashboard tracks traffic, how often the bot understood, ticket speed and leads.
  • Website first, with WhatsApp and Messenger adapters built around the same core.

Status

In development, with all answer content in legal review before launch.
