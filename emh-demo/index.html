Lusaka Securities Exchange · Weak-form efficiency

Does LUSE follow a
random walk?

If a market is weak-form efficient, past prices can’t predict future ones — returns should wander randomly. This lab puts that claim to the test on ~four years of daily closing prices, using three statistical tests and the real estimates from the study.
