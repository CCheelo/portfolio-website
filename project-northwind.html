NorthWind Sales Dashboard

2026 · FSI Outsourcing training Excel · XLOOKUP · Charts Complete

A sales performance dashboard in Excel for the fictional NorthWind Outfitters: actual sales against target, breakdowns by category and salesperson, and a lookup panel that answers “how is this person or product doing?” in one click. Rebuilt here as a live dashboard from the workbook's own data.

What's in it

  • A single-screen Dashboard: January sales of 9,822 against a target of 10,505.8, with month, region and category slicers, sales by category, product margins from the product dimension, and a salesperson leaderboard.
  • A Lookup Panel — pick a salesperson and a product, and XLOOKUP returns their sales, category and performance against target.
  • A tidy model underneath: monthly sales sheets combined into one table, a product dimension and a targets table, so new months drop in without rewiring the charts.
  • Data cleaning — February arrived with lower-case product IDs, misspelt customers and salespeople, a duplicate order ID, impossible dates, a blank customer and a product missing from the product list. The live version shows every one being caught and fixed.

What it practised

Building for the person who will read it: one screen, the headline number first, and a way to answer the obvious follow-up question without opening another sheet. It is the same habit behind the executive and board packs I now produce at AB Bank.
